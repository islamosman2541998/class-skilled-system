# Stage 4 — Site Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Daily site reports from the phone in one step (work done, stage progress, labour, obstacles, photos and video) with the days without a report shown, and progress claims for projects billed by claims: contract and variation quantities, previous / current / cumulative, retention and advance recovery, approval that creates the tax invoice, and the claim PDF.

**Architecture:** Same module pattern as Stages 1–3 (model, Form Requests, thin controller, `DataTable`, Blade, feature tests). A new `SiteServiceProvider` (in `bootstrap/providers.php`, after `FinanceServiceProvider`) joins the registries: permissions, menu, project tabs, report and dashboard figure, invoice hooks. Stage progress rules move out of `ProjectStageController` into one service so the stage form and the daily report change stages the same way. Claim money is computed by one calculator from the lines, the invoice is made from the claim's net with `InvoiceCalculator::fromLines`, and the claim follows its invoice through the Stage 3 issue / cancel hooks.

**Tech Stack:** As Stages 0–3 (mPDF for the claim PDF, the Stage 0 uploader for photos and video). No new package.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/2026-11-12-class-skilled-system-design.md` §8.13 (daily reports), §8.14 (claims), §8.6 (project tabs), §8.7 (stage progress), §8.10 (invoice linked to a claim), §4.4 (engineer phone screens), §5.4 (photos and video), §5.10 (claim PDF and WhatsApp), §9.1 (engineer: "reports not sent"), §14 (unit test for the claim).

## Global Constraints

- Everything in `CLAUDE.md` → Global Constraints applies unchanged. No new composer or npm package.
- Money `decimal(14,2)`, quantities `decimal(12,3)`, rates `decimal(5,2)`, the contract discount rate `decimal(7,4)`; totals on the server with `bcmath` and `QuotationCalculator::round` (half up). The VAT rate is copied into each claim when it starts (§5.9).
- Claim numbers from `NumberGenerator` (`DocumentType::Claim`, prefix from the numbering settings) reserved **at submission**, not at draft (§5.9).
- An approved claim is never edited. Draft claims and daily reports soft delete; submitted, approved and invoiced claims cannot be deleted.
- Every new lang group (`site`) goes into `TranslationCatalog::GROUPS`. Replies to the user in Arabic.
- Commit at the end of every task with the message shown. Local commits only.
- Out of this stage: retention release at handover, suppliers, purchases, subcontracts, labour attendance and equipment (Stage 5). The daily report labour figure is a count, not attendance.

## Business rules this plan fixes

- One daily report per project per day (§8.13 "تقرير لكل مشروع في اليوم"). The author is the signed-in user ("المهندس"). Reports can be written for today or a past day, never a future one.
- A report sets new progress for the stages worked on. A stage takes the progress of its **latest-dated** report, so a report written later for an earlier day never lowers the stage below what a newer report set. Status follows progress as today (100 means done, any progress means started); `actual_start` / `actual_end` take the report date. Deleting a report leaves the stages as they are.
- Days without a report: for projects `in_progress`, each day from `max(start_date, from)` to `min(to, yesterday)` that is not a weekly day off and has no report. Weekly days off come from a new setting `SystemSettings::weekly_days_off` (ISO weekday numbers, default `[5]` = Friday). Today never counts as missing.
- A project billed by claims (`BillingMethod::Claims`, chosen at creation) has no payment schedule: conversion from a quotation copies the items but not the payments, an approved variation adds no installment, the payments tab is hidden and the claims tab is shown instead. Projects billed by installments never show the claims tab.
- Claim lines are the project items (contract) and the items of approved variations. Amounts are before VAT. Quantities are entered as the **current** quantity or as the **cumulative percent** of the contract quantity; the server stores quantities. `previous` = the cumulative quantity of the line on the latest approved or invoiced claim of the project. `|cumulative| ≤ |contract|` and with the same sign (variation lines can be negative).
- Claim money, in order (all rounded half up per step):
  - `work_current` = Σ round(current quantity × unit price); `work_cumulative` likewise.
  - `discount` = round(work_current × `projects.contract_discount_percent` ÷ 100) — the quotation discount as a share of its subtotal, copied at conversion (0 for direct projects).
  - `gross` = work_current − discount.
  - `retention` = round(gross × `projects.retention_percent` ÷ 100). Default retention for new projects from a new `FinanceSettings::retention_percent` (default `5.00`).
  - `advance_recovery` = min(round(gross × `advance_amount` ÷ contract net), advance not yet recovered), where contract net = Σ contract line amounts − the same discount share; when the claim brings every line to its contract quantity, recovery = all of the advance not yet recovered. `projects.advance_amount` is the advance paid before work, before VAT (0 when none); the advance itself is invoiced as an ordinary Stage 3 invoice.
  - `net` = gross − retention − advance_recovery; VAT and total from `InvoiceCalculator::fromLines([net line], vat_rate)`, so the claim total equals its invoice total.
- Claim status: `draft → submitted → approved → invoiced`. Submitting reserves the number and freezes the quantities; the approver may return a submitted claim to draft with a note. Approving creates the draft invoice (`invoices.claim_id`); issuing that invoice marks the claim invoiced; cancelling it returns the claim to approved, and a replacement invoice keeps `claim_id`. One open claim (draft or submitted) per project at a time.
- Permissions: module `daily_reports` = ACTIONS; module `claims` = `view`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `export`, `print`, `approve`. Claim money is shown with the claim (it is the client's bill), like invoices.

## Review Focus

1. Two daily reports for the same project and day at the same moment (two engineers, a double tap) leave one report; the second sees a message with a link to the existing one. [Task 1]
2. A report saved later for an earlier day, or an edit to an old report, never moves a stage backwards over a newer report; deleting a report does not change stages. [Task 1]
3. A cumulative quantity beyond the contract quantity, a negative cumulative on a positive line, or a second open claim (two "start claim" clicks) is refused; previous quantities come only from approved or invoiced claims. [Task 4]
4. Advance recovery never exceeds the advance and the final claim recovers exactly what is left; the claim total equals its invoice total when the steps round (net 10,000.01 at 5%). [Task 4]
5. Approving a claim twice (double click, two tabs) creates one invoice; cancelling that invoice returns the claim to approved and the replacement keeps the claim link. [Task 5]
